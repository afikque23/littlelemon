About Little Lemon

Our Story

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

The chefs Mario and Adrian draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and have a menu of 12-15 items that they rotate seasonally.

The restaurant has a rustic and relaxed atmosphere with moderate prices, making it a popular place for a meal any time of the day.

Mario & Adrian

Brothers and founders bringing the authentic flavours of the Mediterranean straight to your plate.
